For a curious observer of mobility, and of course for decision-making in any level of administration, it is interesting to know how mobility flows work. This is the reason why MITMA releases seasonly mobile phone registers translated in pairs of any flux between different districts.

Interactive visor across MITMA phone data and modal share in each municipality of Madrid Region through declared preferences in 2018 regional survey.

In the framework of The Banlieue Gap research project, we aim to represent and outline with data the reality of Madrid Region mobility, with a special focus on metropolitan peripheries. This is part of a project touching accessibility, mobility forecasting and data.

La escala de los flujos de movilidad, y su percepcion por parte del viajero, puede ser muy diferente segun el lugar donde nos encontramos. Esta investigacion parte de la experiencia metropolitana, donde miles de madrileños se exponen a desplazamientos que derivan de una distribucion territorial no siempre eficiente.

Con este visor podemos mapear y comparar, por ejemplo, las 49.400 personas que viajan a diario desde Alcalá de Henares a Madrid. Que de ellas, podemos estimar que 30.000 lo hacen en coche y 18.000 en transporte público. ¿Son muchas, pocas?. Pues es un tercio de las personas que se desplazan de Madrid a Getafe un dia laborable: cerca de 150.000, de los cuales casi la mitad lo hacen en un medio de transporte público.

Poder visualizar estos flujos es vital para la planificación de la movilidad y para la toma de decisiones operativas del transporte. Es un ejemplo de cómo los datos procedentes de los teléfonos móviles nos permiten transformar el conocimiento en mejores decisiones

Methodological note

Mobility flows come from MITMA mobile phone data, specifically from three representative working days in October 2025 (14th, 15th and 16th), aggregated to the municipal level by combining the constituent districts.

Modal split was estimated from the Madrid Household Mobility Survey (EDM 2018), produced by the Madrid Regional Transport Consortium (CRTM). The shares of car, public transport and soft modes (walking and cycling) were calculated for each origin-destination pair at the transport zone level (ZT1259), then aggregated to the municipal level using a weighted mean by trip volume. Only OD pairs with more than 200 daily trips and sufficient survey representation are shown.

The three modes are defined as: public transport (Cercanías commuter rail, metro, urban and interurban bus, tram and other Renfe services); car (driver and passenger in private, company or rental vehicle, and motorcycle); soft modes (cycling, walking and others).
